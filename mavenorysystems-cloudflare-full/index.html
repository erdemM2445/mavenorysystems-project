FAQ

Clear expectations before purchase.

Is this only for Etsy?

No. The wording is Etsy-friendly, but the logic works for any small shop that can provide product costs, labor time and sales rows.

Does it automatically import CSV files?

The current version includes a CSV paste/staging workflow. It is not a direct marketplace integration.

What decisions does it produce?

Keep, Raise Price, Restock, Stop, Test More and Order Now signals.

Is this accounting software?

No. It is an operational planning and decision tool. It does not replace bookkeeping, tax advice or accounting.
